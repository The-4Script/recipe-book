# Food Storage Guide

Practical tips for storing common food items safely and keeping them fresh for longer.

## General Storage Tips

* **Store dairy products in the refrigerator:** Keep milk, yogurt, cheese, and other dairy products refrigerated and return them to the refrigerator promptly after use.

* **Freeze meat and seafood:** Store meat, poultry, and seafood in the freezer when they will not be used within a few days. Use airtight containers or freezer-safe bags to reduce freezer burn.

* **Keep fruits and vegetables appropriately:** Store most leafy vegetables and berries in the refrigerator, while items such as bananas, onions, potatoes, and garlic are generally better kept in a cool, dry place.

* **Store dry foods at room temperature:** Keep rice, pasta, flour, cereals, and other dry goods in airtight containers in a cool, dry pantry away from moisture and direct sunlight.

* **Refrigerate leftovers promptly:** Place cooked leftovers in clean, covered containers and refrigerate them within two hours of cooking or serving.

* **Keep bread properly stored:** Store bread at room temperature in a sealed bag or container for short-term use. Freeze extra bread if it will not be consumed soon.

* **Use airtight containers for opened foods:** Transfer opened dry foods, snacks, and similar products into airtight containers to protect them from moisture, pests, and stale air.

## Storage Safety

* **Check expiration and use-by dates:** Regularly inspect stored foods and use older items first to reduce unnecessary waste.

* **Keep raw and cooked foods separate:** Store raw meat and seafood separately from ready-to-eat foods to reduce the risk of cross-contamination.

* **Label stored food:** Mark containers with the storage date, especially for leftovers and frozen foods, so older items can be identified easily.

* **Keep storage areas clean:** Regularly clean refrigerators, freezers, and pantry shelves to prevent spills, odors, pests, and contamination.
