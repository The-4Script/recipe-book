# Paneer Butter Masala

A rich, creamy, and mildly spiced North Indian curry made with paneer (Indian cottage cheese), tomatoes, cashews, and butter.

---

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| **Difficulty** | Medium |
| **Prep Time** | 15 mins |
| **Cook Time** | 25 mins |
| **Total Time** | 40 mins |
| **Servings** | 4 |


## Ingredients

### For the Curry Base
* **2 tbsp** Butter
* **1 tbsp** Oil
* **1 medium** Onion, roughly chopped
* **4 large** Tomatoes, chopped
* **10-12** Whole Cashews
* **1 inch** Ginger, chopped
* **4-5 cloves** Garlic
* **1** Green Cardamom pod
* **1 small piece** Cinnamon stick

### Spices & Seasoning
* **1 tsp** Kashmiri Red Chili Powder
* **1/2 tsp** Garam Masala
* **1/2 tsp** Coriander Powder
* **1/2 tsp** Kasuri Methi (dried fenugreek leaves), crushed
* **To taste** Salt
* **1 tsp** Sugar or Honey

### Main & Finish
* **250g** Paneer, cut into cubes
* **2 tbsp** Fresh Heavy Cream
* **2 tbsp** Fresh Coriander, chopped (for garnish)
* **1 tbsp** Butter (for finishing)

---

## Preparation Steps

1. **Sauté Aromatics:** Heat 1 tbsp butter and 1 tbsp oil in a deep pan. Add cardamom, cinnamon, onion, ginger, and garlic. Cook until onions turn translucent.
2. **Prepare Tomato-Cashew Mixture:** Add chopped tomatoes and cashews. Cook on medium heat for 7-8 minutes until the tomatoes soften completely.
3. **Blend the Sauce:** Allow the mixture to cool slightly, then blend it with 1/2 cup of water into a silky smooth paste. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve for a velvet-smooth restaurant texture (optional).
4. **Simmer the Gravy:** Melt 1 tbsp of butter in the pan on low heat. Add Kashmiri red chili powder (this enhances the rich red color without burning). Pour in the blended tomato-cashew puree. Mix well, cover, and simmer for 5-7 minutes.
5. **Add Spices & Paneer:** Stir in coriander powder, garam masala, salt, and sugar. Mix thoroughly. Add the paneer cubes and let them simmer in the sauce for 3-4 minutes to absorb the flavors.
6. **Finish & Serve:** Stir in cream, crushed Kasuri Methi, and the remaining 1 tbsp of butter. Remove from heat. Garnish with chopped coriander and serve hot with naan, roti, or jeera rice.
